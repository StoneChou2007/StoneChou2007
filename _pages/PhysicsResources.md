### My thoughts on the references

QFT is notoriously confusing for beginners, and unfortunately no single textbook is especially reader-friendly. My strong recommendation is **Zamolodchikov's notes** — despite their occasional flaws, they focus on the *logic* and *physics* of QFT rather than getting buried in algebra. That makes them harder going at first, but more natural in the long run.

For complementary perspectives:

- **Itzykson & Zuber, *Quantum Field Theory*** — excellent for the algebraic details Zamolodchikov sometimes glosses over.
- **Schwartz, *Quantum Field Theory and the Standard Model*** — popular and accessible. Many people start here; personally I think it sacrifices some depth, but to each their own.
