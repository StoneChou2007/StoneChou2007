## comments ##
For beginners you will find this topic extremely confusing, and as always, no textbook is actually written in a very reader-friendly way,
and I highly recommended to read Zamolodchikov'c Note, despite the possible flaws in it. The reason you will find after you really understand 
QFT: this textbook mainly focus on the logic and physics side of QFT rather than its annoying algebra, which makes it harder to read but logically more 
natural. I would also recommend Quantum Field Theory by Itzykson and Zuber, that book persuade algebraic details quantastically. Of course you guys probably want to read
Schwartz and I have nothing against that ---- just a matter of taste, though I would say you have a really bad taste. Also, the little book diagrammar is listed here.
I find it particularly useful when I am doing loop correction;
