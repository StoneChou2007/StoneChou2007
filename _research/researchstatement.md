## Research Statement

I am a senior undergraduate student at Stony Brook University and I have many past research experiences. I worked with Neutrino and Nucleon Decay group and designed an innovative and effective way of atmospheric neutrino reconstruction which could be implemented in the next generation neutrino detectors. I participated in the Technology development and upgrade of Deep Under Ground Neutrino experiment, also known as DUNE detector. After that, I was supervised under Professor Verbaarschot toward a numerical method of Quantum Chaos and SYK model.

Now I am interested in the intersection of Quantum Information, Condense matter and Quantum many-body theory. I am developing an Dynamic Automorphism code for universal quantum computing and quantum error correction. I am also remaining highly relavant to the work about quantum phase transition and Strong-to-Weak Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking, Also known as SWSSB.
